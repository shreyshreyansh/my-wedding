# Research, October 2026: art and story for "two cultures meeting"

The couple asked for art that tells their story: his Bihar, her Marathi and Buddhist Maharashtra, and how they meet.

- `06-story-customs-bridges.md`: customs for each chapter (Bihari, Marathi, Marathi Buddhist), the true links between the two homes, options for the opening blessing, draft caption lines, and questions for the families. Every claim has a source and a grade.
- `06-art-candidates.json`: about 90 openly licensed artworks, photographs, textiles, objects and botanical plates, from three research tracks (Bihar; Maharashtra and its Buddhist heritage; things beyond paintings). Each entry has its source page, full-size image link, licence and where the licence is stated, size, what it shows, details to zoom into, and concerns.
- `06-board-curation.json`: the shortlist shown to the couple on the research board, chapter by chapter.

Nothing here is on the site yet. Before a work is used, recheck its licence on the holding museum's own page, then add it to `src/data/art.json` and to `docs/05-art-sources.md`.
